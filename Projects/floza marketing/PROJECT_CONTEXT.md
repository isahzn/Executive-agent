# Floza — Creative Project Context

## Brand

* Name: Floza
* Type: AI automation platform for businesses
* Goal: Help businesses automate repetitive tasks using AI.
* Main audience: Small and medium-sized businesses.
* Brand should feel like a real, modern SaaS company.

## Current Goal

We are creating Instagram marketing content for Floza, especially faceless Reels and promotional posters.

The content should look realistic and believable. Do not invent fake customers, fake results, or fake usage statistics.

## Reel Visuals

Whenever possible, use:

* Realistic business environments
* Realistic laptops, phones, dashboards and websites
* Actual Floza UI when available
* Screen recordings
* Simple animations
* Natural lighting
* Professional SaaS design

Avoid:

* AI-looking humans
* Robots
* Holograms
* Excessive neon
* Unrealistic futuristic interfaces
* Fake testimonials
* Fake statistics

## Intentional “Before” Mockups

For marketing videos, we may intentionally create ugly, outdated, messy or inefficient-looking websites/dashboards to demonstrate the problem Floza solves.

When I ask for an **“ugly dashboard,” “messy website,” “bad UI,” “outdated website,” or similar**, make it intentionally poor:

* Cluttered layout
* Bad spacing
* Too many colors
* Outdated design
* Excessive buttons
* Poor typography
* Confusing navigation
* Amateur-looking UI

These mockups are fictional visual props for advertisements. They should NOT be presented as real businesses or real customer interfaces.

## Important

When I ask you to create something for this project, follow the specific instructions in my current request over this general context.

Prioritize realistic visuals and believable business scenarios.
